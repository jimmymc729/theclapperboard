Your Last Trip To The Theater In 2026 Is About To Get Very, Very Crowded

Four months of blockbuster fatigue are ending in the best possible way — here's every confirmed late-2026 tentpole with a trailer already out, ranked by how hard they've already broken the internet.

1

Robert Downey Jr. Is Back In The MCU — And He's The Villain Now

The Russo brothers are reuniting basically every hero Marvel has ever put on screen for one collision course, and the trailers have made it very clear Doctor Doom isn't messing around. It's the culmination of years of teasers, and the cast list alone reads like a con-floor fever dream.

2

Dune Is Ending, And Paul Atreides Has Never Looked More Broken

Denis Villeneuve is closing out his trilogy with the story of what happens after the messiah wins, and the trailers have leaned hard into that darker, guiltier tone. Fans in the YouTube comments are already calling for awards attention before anyone's even seen the finished film.

3

DC Made A Batman Movie That's Actually A Body-Horror Film, And It Looks Unhinged

Clayface takes one of Batman's weirder villains and turns his origin into full-blown practical-effects horror, with a script from horror veteran Mike Flanagan. The trailer's already infamous for one specific airplane bathroom scene that reportedly made the crew look away during filming.

4

We're Getting Teenage Haymitch, And It's Going To Hurt

Sunrise on the Reaping goes back 24 years before Katniss to show how a young Haymitch Abernathy survived the brutal 50th Games, with a stacked ensemble stepping into iconic roles. It's the origin story of exactly how cynical and broken Haymitch became by the time the original trilogy starts.

5

Brad Bird Sat On This Idea For 30 Years — Now It's Finally Happening

Ray Gunn is the animation legend's long-shelved noir detective passion project, revived after decades in development purgatory. It's landing on Netflix the same night as two of the biggest theatrical releases of the year, which says something about how much confidence Netflix has in it.
