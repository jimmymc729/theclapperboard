1

Doctor Doom Just Walked Into The MCU — And The Whole Internet Felt It

Robert Downey Jr. is back in the Marvel universe, except now he's playing the villain, and the first trailer confirmed it in the most theatrical way possible. The Russo brothers are directing this ensemble reunion of Avengers, X-Men, and Fantastic Four characters, and it's shaping up to be the biggest crossover the MCU has ever attempted.

2

Shrek Is Back. Donkey Is Back. We're Emotionally Compromised.

Fifteen-plus years after Shrek Forever After, the swamp is reopening. Universal's first teaser confirmed the original trio is fully intact for a new story, with a grown-up twist: Shrek and Fiona's kids are joining the adventure, and the film has already been bumped from its original date to June 30, 2027.

3

Gotham's Getting Colder — And Robert Pattinson Never Left

Matt Reeves finally gave fans a first look at his long-delayed sequel, and it's dark, snowy, and stacked with new faces trading their superhero capes for Gotham's rogues gallery. The film has been bumped multiple times and is now locked for February 18, 2028, but the first camera-test footage of Pattinson back in the suit already has people counting down.

4

Arrakis Isn't Done With Paul Atreides Yet

Denis Villeneuve is closing out his trilogy by adapting Dune Messiah, jumping the story nearly two decades ahead as Emperor Paul faces a conspiracy that could unravel everything he built. The trailer confirmed major returning stars alongside a wild new addition — Robert Pattinson playing a shape-shifting Face Dancer villain — and it's set to go head-to-head with Avengers: Doomsday on the very same December weekend.
