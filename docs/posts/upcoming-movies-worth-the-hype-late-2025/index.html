5 Movies That Turned Late 2025 Into An Absolute Fever Dream Of A Movie Season

From Pandora's volcanoes to Oz's final act to a Predator who's suddenly the good guy — here's why these five had everyone losing it.

1

James Cameron Went Back To Pandora AGAIN — And This Time He's Bringing The Fire

James Cameron returns to direct the third chapter of his sci-fi saga, bringing back Sam Worthington and Zoe Saldaña as Jake Sully and Neytiri as they face off against a terrifying new tribe: the Ash People. It hit theaters December 19, 2025, nearly three years to the day after The Way of Water, and clocks in at over three hours of pure Pandora chaos.

2

The Wicked Saga Finally Sticks The Landing — And It's Been A Whole Year Coming

Jon M. Chu's two-part adaptation of the Broadway musical wraps up with Cynthia Erivo and Ariana Grande returning as Elphaba and Glinda, this time navigating a world that's turned Elphaba into a villain. Wicked: For Good soared into theaters November 21, 2025 and had already broken pre-sale records before a single ticket got scanned.

3

Nine Years Later, Zootopia Finally Got Its Sequel — And Nick And Judy Are In Couples Counseling

Directors Jared Bush and Byron Howard reunite Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde, now official police partners dealing with growing pains as they chase a mysterious new villain, Gary De'Snake. Zootopia 2 arrived November 26, 2025, nearly a decade after the original became a billion-dollar hit.

4

Wait, We're Rooting FOR The Predator Now? Dan Trachtenberg Flipped The Whole Franchise On Its Head

Prey director Dan Trachtenberg returns with a story that makes a young, outcast Yautja the actual hero, teaming him up with Elle Fanning's android character on a hunt across a deadly alien planet. Trachtenberg even ran the twist by James Cameron himself, who was initially skeptical but told him he'd "pulled it off." Predator: Badlands hit theaters November 7, 2025.

5

Edgar Wright Took On A Stephen King Classic — And Went Closer To The Book Than Arnold Ever Did

Glen Powell stars as Ben Richards, a desperate father who enters a deadly televised game show to save his daughter, in Edgar Wright's remake of the 1982 King novel. The film stays closer to the source material than the 1987 Schwarzenegger version, and even squeezes in an Arnold cameo — as a face on the $100 bill. It landed in theaters November 14, 2025.
