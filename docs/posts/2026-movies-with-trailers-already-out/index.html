5 Sequels We've Been Begging For Are Finally Here (Or About To Be) — And The Trailers Do Not Disappoint

1

Toy Story 5 Just Made 'Toy Meets Tech' Sound Genuinely Terrifying

Woody, Buzz, and Jessie are back, but this time their real threat isn't a kid growing up — it's a tablet. The toys' entire existence gets challenged when Lilypad, a shiny new device voiced by Greta Lee, shows up with her own ideas about what's best for Bonnie.

2

It's Been 16 Years, But Shrek, Donkey, And Fiona Are Finally Getting Back In The Swamp

Mike Myers, Eddie Murphy, and Cameron Diaz are all confirmed to return to their signature roles for the first new Shrek movie since 2010's Shrek Forever After. The real jaw-dropper is the new addition: Zendaya has joined the cast as Felicia, Shrek and Fiona's grown-up daughter, alongside Marcello Hernandez and Skyler Gisondo as her brothers Fergus and Farkle.

3

Milly Alcock's Supergirl Cameo Stole Superman — Now She Gets Her Own Movie

After turning up for a scene-stealing appearance in 2025's Superman, Milly Alcock returns as Kara Zor-El in a film based on Tom King's Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow comic. Directed by Craig Gillespie, the movie brings in Jason Momoa as bounty hunter Lobo and reunites Kara with her flying dog Krypto.

4

The Four Horsemen Are Back After Nearly A Decade Away

Jesse Eisenberg, Woody Harrelson, Dave Franco, Isla Fisher, and Morgan Freeman all returned for the franchise's third installment, the first Now You See Me movie since 2016. They're joined by a new generation of illusionists played by Justice Smith, Dominic Sessa, and Ariana Greenblatt, plus Rosamund Pike as a new adversary.

5

Johnny Cage Finally Shows Up — And The Tournament Actually Happens This Time

Karl Urban steps in as Johnny Cage, a character fans have waited for since he was teased at the end of the 2021 reboot, joining returning fighters like Hiroyuki Sanada's Scorpion, Lewis Tan's Cole Young, and Jessica McNamee's Sonya Blade. Director Simon McQuoid brings Shao Kahn, played by Martyn Ford, in as the new central threat as Earthrealm's champions finally face the real Mortal Kombat tournament.
