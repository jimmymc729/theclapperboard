4 Movies Left In 2026 (And Beyond) That Are About To Wreck Us Emotionally

Witchy sisters, a mask-wearing detective duo, Doctor Doom himself, and one very delayed Bat-sequel — here's everything we're still counting down to.

1

Sally And Gillian Owens Are FINALLY Coming Home

Nearly three decades after the original spellbound us, Sandra Bullock and Nicole Kidman are reuniting as the Owens sisters, and the trailer already has us reaching for tissues and moonlight-appropriate outfits. The sequel pulls from Alice Hoffman's own follow-up novel, with Sally and Gillian facing down the family curse all over again — this time with Joey King along for the ride.

2

Judy And Nick Are Back On The Case — And This Time There's A Snake Involved

Ginnifer Goodwin and Jason Bateman are slipping back into their badges as Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde, and this go-round the ZPD's favorite mismatched duo gets ordered into couples counseling before chasing down a mysterious reptile who turns Zootopia upside down. Ke Huy Quan joins the cast as the venomous-but-maybe-not-so-bad Gary De'Snake, and the trailer promises brand new corners of the city we've never seen.

3

Doctor Doom Has Entered The Chat And The MCU Will Never Be The Same

Robert Downey Jr. is officially back in the MCU — not as Tony Stark, but as the terrifying Victor Von Doom — and the trailer makes clear the Fantastic Four already have a complicated history with him. The Russo brothers are stacking basically every corner of the franchise into one movie, from the Thunderbolts to Fox's original X-Men cast, making this one of the biggest ensemble team-ups Marvel has ever attempted.

4

Yes, It Got Delayed AGAIN — But The First Look At Pattinson's Return Still Broke The Internet

Look, we're not going to pretend The Batman Part II hasn't bounced around the release calendar — it's now landing February 18, 2028 instead of its once-planned 2026 date, giving director Matt Reeves extra time in post. But Robert Pattinson's already teased that the script is "extraordinary," and the first camera-test footage of him back in the cowl, plus new additions like Scarlett Johansson and Sebastian Stan, has fans convinced the wait will be worth it.
